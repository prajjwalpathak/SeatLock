# Monsoon Theatre, Mumbai

## Location and getting there
Monsoon Theatre is at 45 Marine Drive, Churchgate, Mumbai 400020, a 10 minute walk from Churchgate railway station.

## Seating layout
The theatre has 10 rows (A to J) with 20 seats each, 200 seats in total. Rows A to C are Platinum, rows D to G are Gold and rows H to J are Silver.

## Aisle seats
The central aisle is between seats 10 and 11 in every row, so seats 10 and 11 are aisle seats.

## Entry and timings
Doors open 30 minutes before the show. Latecomers are seated at the first suitable break.
