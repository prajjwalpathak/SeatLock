# Riverside Stage, Pune

## Location and getting there
Riverside Stage is at 7 Koregaon Park Road, Pune 411001, about 15 minutes by road from Pune railway station.

## Seating layout
Riverside Stage is the smallest venue: 8 rows (A to H) with 16 seats each, 128 seats in total. Rows A to C are Platinum, rows D to G are Gold and row H is Silver.

## Aisle seats
The central aisle is between seats 8 and 9 in every row, so seats 8 and 9 are aisle seats.

## Entry and timings
Doors open 30 minutes before the show.
