# Banyan Hall, Bengaluru

## Location and getting there
Banyan Hall is at 88 MG Road, Ashok Nagar, Bengaluru 560001, next to the MG Road metro station on the Purple line.

## Seating layout
Banyan Hall is the largest venue: 14 rows (A to N) with 28 seats each, 392 seats in total. Rows A to C are Platinum, rows D to G are Gold and rows H to N are Silver.

## Aisle seats
The central aisle is between seats 14 and 15 in every row, so seats 14 and 15 are aisle seats.

## Entry and timings
Doors open 45 minutes before the show.
