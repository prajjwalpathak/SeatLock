# Terms of booking

## Holding seats
Choosing seats on the seat map and pressing "Hold seats" reserves them for you for 8 minutes. Nobody else can take held seats during that time. When the 8 minutes are over, unpaid held seats are released automatically.

## How many seats you can hold
You can hold up to 6 seats at a time, and only one hold per show. The seats in one hold are reserved all together or not at all: if any of them was just taken by someone else, nothing is held and you can pick again.

## From hold to booking
"Proceed to pay" turns your hold into a booking. The booking keeps the same deadline as the hold: you must pay before the timer on the booking page runs out, otherwise the booking expires and the seats are released.

## Prices
Prices are set per section (Platinum, Gold, Silver) for each show and are shown on the seat map before you hold. The amount you pay is always calculated by SeatLock from the seats in your booking; there are no hidden fees.

## Payment
Payments are processed by Razorpay. SeatLock runs Razorpay in test mode: no real money is charged, and test cards or the test net-banking bank can be used. A booking is confirmed only after the payment has been verified on our server.

## Your ticket
A confirmed booking is your ticket. It shows the show, venue, date and time, your seats and a QR code, and you can download it as an image. Show the booking page or the downloaded ticket at the entry.

## Fair use and limits
To keep flash sales fair, the number of hold attempts per minute is limited for each person and each network. If you see "Too many attempts", wait for the time shown and try again.

## Accounts
You need an account to hold seats and book. You can register with an e-mail and password or sign in with Google. Browsing events and seat maps does not need an account.
