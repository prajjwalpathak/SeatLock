# Frequently asked questions

## Why did my seats disappear while I was choosing?
The seat map is live. If someone else holds or books a seat you had selected, it is removed from your selection and you are told which seats were taken. Pick other seats and hold again.

## My hold timer ran out. What now?
The seats were released and may already be taken. Go back to the seat map, pick seats again and hold them. Nothing was charged.

## I paid but the booking still says payment pending
Sometimes the bank confirms a few seconds later. The page checks again automatically; Razorpay also notifies us directly, so the booking is confirmed even if you closed the browser. If the payment arrived after the booking expired, it is refunded in full automatically.

## Can I change my seats after booking?
Seats cannot be swapped inside a booking. You can cancel the booking (see the refund policy for how much you get back) and book new seats.

## Can I transfer my booking to someone else?
Bookings are linked to the account that made them and cannot be transferred. The person attending can show the downloaded ticket.

## What does the Seat Finder do?
Seat Finder suggests up to three groups of seats next to each other that match what you ask for, such as "2 seats near the aisle under 800 rupees". It only suggests seats; you still choose an option and press Hold. If the assistant is unavailable, the filters below it do the same search.

## Where do I find my bookings?
Open "My bookings" from the account menu. It lists all your bookings, newest first, with their status.

## Is my card data stored by SeatLock?
No. Card and UPI details are entered in Razorpay's checkout and never reach SeatLock's servers. We only store the payment reference and status.
