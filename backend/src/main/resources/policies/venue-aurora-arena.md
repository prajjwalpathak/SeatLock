# Aurora Arena, Delhi

## Location and getting there
Aurora Arena is at 12 Ring Road, Lajpat Nagar, New Delhi 110024. The nearest metro station is Lajpat Nagar on the Violet and Pink lines, about a 7 minute walk.

## Seating layout
The arena has 12 rows (A to L) with 24 seats each, 288 seats in total. Rows A to C are Platinum, rows D to G are Gold and rows H to L are Silver. Row A is closest to the stage.

## Aisle seats
Every row has one central aisle between seats 12 and 13, so seats 12 and 13 in each row are aisle seats.

## Entry and timings
Doors open 45 minutes before the show. Late entry is allowed only between acts.
