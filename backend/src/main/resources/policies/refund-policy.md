# Cancellation and refund policy

## Cancelling a paid booking
You can cancel a confirmed (paid) booking yourself from the booking page with "Cancel booking". The whole booking is cancelled: all of its seats are released together. Cancelling only some of the seats in a booking is not possible.

## How much you get back
The refund depends on how long before the show starts you cancel. At least 48 hours before the show: a full refund, 100 % of what you paid. At least 24 hours but less than 48 hours before the show: 50 % of what you paid. Amounts are rounded down to the paisa.

## When cancellation closes
Less than 24 hours before the show starts, a paid booking can no longer be cancelled and nothing is refunded. Once the show has started, a booking can never be cancelled.

## How long a refund takes
Refunds go back to the original payment method (card, UPI or net banking) through Razorpay. We send the refund to Razorpay within a few minutes of the cancellation; the bank usually shows it within 5 to 7 working days. If sending fails, we retry automatically.

## Seats after a cancellation
As soon as a booking is cancelled, its seats are available to everyone again and appear as free on the live seat map.

## Cancelling before you pay
A booking that has not been paid yet can be cancelled at any time before it expires, at no cost, because nothing was charged. Its seats are released immediately.

## Payments that arrive too late
If your payment is completed after your booking has already expired (the payment window closed), the seats may have gone to someone else. In that case we do not confirm the booking and refund the full amount automatically. You do not need to contact us.

## Paying twice by mistake
If the same booking is paid twice, the booking is confirmed once and the second payment is refunded in full automatically.
